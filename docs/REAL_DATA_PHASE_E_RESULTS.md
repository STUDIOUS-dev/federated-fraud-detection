# Phase E on real data: OpenML credit-card (E5)

_Generated by `python -m src.experiments.real_data_e` (aggregation: `src/experiments/real_data_e_report.py`). Do not edit by hand. Interpretation: `docs/FINAL_REPORT.md`, section 7.5._

Each cell is **mean ± std over 5 seeds**. Data and banks are those of the frozen real-data study (`docs/REAL_DATA_RESULTS.md`): temporal split by row order (80/20), training rows assigned at random to 4 banks holding 45 / 30 / 15 / 10% of the rows with an unequal fraud share, features standardised on the training split. The test split has only **75 frauds**, so differences between healthy methods are noisy. 10 rounds, 2 local epochs, the same MLP and the same clients and Tracked* strategies as the synthetic Phase E study. Baselines are re-run here (in-process runner) and can differ slightly from the frozen real-data study, which used its own aggregation loop.

Attackers: the single scaling attacker is bank_a (the largest bank, as in the frozen study) and sends its weights × −10 (the synthetic study's attack; the frozen real-data study scaled the update by +10 instead). The two adaptive attackers are bank_c and bank_d (25% of the rows, 2 of 4 clients). FedTrimmedAvg is omitted: it gave exactly the same numbers as FedMedian in every synthetic Phase E scenario.
